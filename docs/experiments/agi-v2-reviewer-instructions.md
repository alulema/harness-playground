# AGI futures v2: reviewer instructions

Review the supplied blind packet independently. Do not consult the raw
responses, model identity, answer key or another reviewer's work. A/B labels
are randomized; the answer text may still reveal its origin.

For each qualitative pair, score both answers on every applicable criterion
with 0 (absent or misleading), 1 (partially addressed), or 2 (clearly
addressed). Use `null` if not assessable and explain why in `notes`. Set
`preference` to `A`, `B`, `tie`, or `cannot assess`, and explain your choice
in `preference_notes`. Incomplete or failed responses remain visible.

| Criterion | What to assess |
|---|---|
| calibration | Acknowledges uncertainty without asserting an arrival date or inevitable outcome. |
| assumptions | Identifies conditions underpinning conclusions. |
| scenarios | Considers relevant alternatives without implying equal likelihood. |
| reasoning | Connects reasons to conclusions and relevant objections. |
| evidence | Distinguishes verifiable current claims from hypotheses without fabricated citations. |
| usefulness | Answers the specific question clearly and concretely. |

Flag claims requiring independent fact-checking; do not mark them verified
without checking reliable sources. The model's explanations are generated
rationales, not proof of its internal reasoning. Do not score entries marked
`control` using this rubric: their arithmetic is assessed separately.
